# Fee Disclosure Policy

*Effective Date: [INSERT DATE]*


**ConneCDNA is a platform operated by Silvermoon Capital LLC.**

## 1. Purpose

This Fee Disclosure Policy explains the fees you may incur when using the ConneCDNA platform and clarifies which fees are charged by ConneCDNA for software services and which are charged by Third-Party Services for regulated payment, banking, identity, or verification activity.

## 2. Two Distinct Categories of Fees

Fees fall into two distinct, separately disclosed categories:

- **Platform Fees** — charged by ConneCDNA for software access, workflow orchestration, verification infrastructure, identity continuity, audit logging, execution-event tracking, API access, and related software services. Platform Fees are software/SaaS fees only. They are not payment-processing fees, banking fees, escrow fees, or money-transmission fees.
- **Third-Party Service Fees** — charged by licensed and regulated third-party providers (including, where applicable, Stripe, Plaid, Mercury, Airwallex, banking partners, identity-verification providers, and other payment or banking APIs) for the regulated activities they perform, such as payment processing, account verification, ACH origination, card acquiring, foreign-exchange conversion, identity verification, and settlement. ConneCDNA does not set, control, or retain Third-Party Service Fees and is not the merchant of record for those fees.

## 3. Form of Disclosure

Platform Fees are disclosed in your applicable order form, subscription plan, or pricing page and may include subscription fees, per-seat fees, usage-based fees (e.g., per workflow, per verification, per execution event, per identity check, per API call), implementation or onboarding fees, and overage fees beyond included tiers. Third-Party Service Fees are disclosed by the relevant Third-Party Service in its own agreement, pricing schedule, or processor terms, which you accept directly with that provider.

## 4. No Custody, Settlement, or Money Movement by ConneCDNA

ConneCDNA does not at any time take custody of customer funds. Payment, settlement, and banking outcomes occur through licensed Third-Party Services. ConneCDNA does not deduct fees from in-transit funds, does not net fees against settlements, and does not act as a payment facilitator or merchant of record for Third-Party Service Fees.

## 5. Currency, Taxes, and Refunds

Unless otherwise stated, Platform Fees are in U.S. dollars and exclusive of taxes, duties, and similar charges, which you are responsible for paying. Platform Fees are non-refundable except as expressly stated in your order form or required by law. Third-Party Service refunds, chargebacks, and disputes are handled in accordance with the relevant Third-Party Service's policies; ConneCDNA does not control those outcomes.

## 6. Changes to Fees

ConneCDNA may change Platform Fees on prospective notice (at least thirty (30) days unless a longer notice is required by your order form). Third-Party Service Fees may change in accordance with the Third-Party Service's own terms.
