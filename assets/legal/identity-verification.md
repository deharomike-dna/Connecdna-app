# Identity Verification & Compliance Disclosure


**ConneCDNA is a platform operated by Silvermoon Capital LLC.**

## 1. Role of ConneCDNA in Identity Verification

ConneCDNA provides identity-verification orchestration as part of its software platform. Where customer workflows require identity verification, KYC, KYB, document verification, sanctions screening, or similar checks, the underlying verification is performed by a licensed third-party identity provider. ConneCDNA does not act as the verifying institution and does not make regulated determinations of identity, sanctions status, or KYC sufficiency.

## 2. Customer Compliance Responsibility

Customers are responsible for designing and operating their own AML, KYC, KYB, sanctions, and consumer-protection programs in accordance with applicable law (including the Bank Secrecy Act, OFAC, FinCEN, and applicable state laws). ConneCDNA's identity, verification, and audit features are software tools that support a customer compliance program; they are not a substitute for it and do not transfer regulatory obligations to ConneCDNA.

## 3. Data Handling for Verification

Identity-verification data is processed in accordance with the Privacy Policy, the Data Usage & Audit Logging Disclosure, and the relevant identity provider's policies.

## 4. Limitations

- Verification outcomes reflect the determination of the third-party provider; ConneCDNA does not override those outcomes.
- Identity verification is probabilistic and is not a guarantee of identity, eligibility, sanctions status, or future behavior.
- Customers are responsible for human review where regulation or context requires it.

## 5. Disclosures to End Users

Customers must provide appropriate notices and obtain appropriate consents from end users regarding identity verification, including disclosures required by applicable law and by the third-party identity provider.
