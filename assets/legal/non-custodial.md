# Non-Custodial & Non-Money-Transmission Disclosure


**ConneCDNA is a platform operated by Silvermoon Capital LLC.**

## 1. Purpose

This Disclosure provides standardized non-custodial and non-money-transmission language used across the ConneCDNA platform, customer-facing materials, marketing collateral, dashboards, agreements, and investor communications. Consistent use of this language is an operational safeguard supporting ConneCDNA's classification as a software/SaaS infrastructure provider rather than a regulated financial-services entity.

## 2. Core Disclosure (Long Form)

> *ConneCDNA is a software platform owned and licensed by Silvermoon Capital LLC. ConneCDNA provides workflow orchestration, verification infrastructure, identity continuity, audit logging, and execution-readiness tooling. ConneCDNA is not a bank, money transmitter, money-services business, escrow agent, payment processor, payment facilitator, broker-dealer, investment adviser, or fiduciary. ConneCDNA does not at any time take possession, custody, or control of customer funds. All payment, banking, settlement, and identity-verification activity occurs through licensed and regulated third-party providers, including, where applicable, Stripe, Plaid, Mercury, Airwallex, and other banking and payment APIs.*

## 3. Short-Form Disclosure (UX Tooltip / Footer)

> *ConneCDNA is software infrastructure. Payments, banking, and settlement are performed by licensed third-party providers. ConneCDNA does not hold customer funds.*

## 4. Investor / Diligence Statement

> *ConneCDNA is operated as a software-as-a-service business. Silvermoon Capital LLC owns the underlying intellectual property, brand, infrastructure, trust, execution, orchestration, identity, and API frameworks. ConneCDNA does not maintain pooled or for-the-benefit-of accounts, does not initiate or settle transfers from accounts it controls, and does not hold customer funds or assets. Regulated financial activity is performed exclusively by licensed third-party providers.*

## 5. Required Avoidances

ConneCDNA materials must not contain language suggesting any of the following without correction:

- that ConneCDNA "holds," "stores," or "safeguards" customer funds;
- that ConneCDNA provides "accounts," "balances," "deposits," or "wallets";
- that ConneCDNA "settles" or "guarantees settlement" of any transaction;
- that ConneCDNA is "FDIC-insured" or that customer funds are insured (without disclosing the actual insured institution and its terms);
- that ConneCDNA is "licensed" as a bank, money transmitter, or money-services business;
- that ConneCDNA acts as a "fiduciary," "trustee," or "custodian" for any user;
- that ConneCDNA provides "investment advice," "brokerage," or "trading services."

## 6. Approved Vocabulary

In place of regulated-finance language, materials should use:

- "software platform," "infrastructure," "orchestration," "workflow," "verification," "identity continuity," "audit log," "execution-event log," "compliance evidence trail," "API framework," and "execution intelligence layer."

## 7. Operational Use

The Long-Form Disclosure appears in the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy, the Fee Disclosure Policy, the Payment Processor Integration Terms, and any enterprise or investor agreement. The Short-Form Disclosure appears in product UX surfaces where money-related actions occur (for example, near payment-initiation or verification controls). The Investor / Diligence Statement is used in fundraising materials and data rooms.
