# Platform Risk Allocation & Liability Limitation Framework


**ConneCDNA is a platform operated by Silvermoon Capital LLC.**

## 1. Allocation Principles

Risk under the Platform is allocated based on each party's ability to control and price the relevant risk. Customers control their data, configurations, end-user relationships, business activity, and regulatory programs. Third-Party Services control regulated payment, banking, and identity activity. ConneCDNA controls the software, orchestration, and audit infrastructure.

## 2. Risks Borne by Customer

- lawfulness and accuracy of Customer Data and end-user relationships;
- configuration and business decisions made on the Platform;
- regulatory authorizations applicable to the customer's business;
- compliance with Third-Party Service terms and Acceptable Use Policy;
- end-user disclosures and consents.

## 3. Risks Borne by Third-Party Services

- payment processing, settlement, and chargeback handling;
- banking, custody, and money movement;
- regulated identity verification and KYC determinations;
- compliance with money-transmission, banking, and identity-verification regulation in their domains.

## 4. Risks Addressed by ConneCDNA

- availability, reliability, and security of the software platform consistent with stated SLAs;
- orchestration accuracy according to documented behavior;
- audit-log integrity and access controls;
- protection of intellectual property of Silvermoon Capital LLC.

## 5. Limitation of Liability

Liability for use of the Platform is limited as set forth in Section 16 of the Terms of Service. The aggregate liability of ConneCDNA and Silvermoon Capital LLC is capped at the greater of the fees paid by the Customer in the preceding twelve (12) months or US$100. Indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, and punitive damages are excluded. These limitations apply to all theories of liability and even if a remedy fails of its essential purpose.

## 6. Excluded Liabilities

ConneCDNA and Silvermoon Capital LLC are not liable for: (i) Processor decisions, downtime, or errors; (ii) settlement outcomes, chargebacks, or reversals; (iii) third-party identity-verification determinations; (iv) Customer's regulatory compliance; (v) decisions made by Customers using Platform outputs; (vi) loss of profits, data, or goodwill; or (vii) force-majeure events.

## 7. Insurance

ConneCDNA maintains insurance coverage appropriate to a software/SaaS business of its size and industry. Customers are responsible for their own insurance coverage suitable to their business activities.

## 8. Indemnities

Indemnification obligations are as set forth in Section 17 of the Terms of Service.

## 9. IP Risk Allocation

All right, title, and interest in the ConneCDNA name, brand, infrastructure, trust infrastructure, execution systems, orchestration systems, identity systems, API frameworks, software, and related intellectual property are owned by Silvermoon Capital LLC. Customers receive only the limited license expressly stated in the Terms of Service. Silvermoon Capital LLC reserves all rights not expressly granted, including the right to enforce its intellectual-property rights against unauthorized use.

## 10. Survival

Provisions that by their nature should survive termination — including intellectual-property ownership, confidentiality, disclaimers, limitations of liability, indemnities, governing law, arbitration, and this risk-allocation framework — survive termination of any agreement governing the Platform.
